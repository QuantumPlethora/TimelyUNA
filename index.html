Chapter four • Cosmic Life

What can a star become?

“A black hole is a star so bright it forgot to let go of its light.”

A black hole does not shine like an ordinary star. Its gravity bends spacetime so strongly that light crossing the event horizon cannot return to the outside universe.

Tap a stage to reveal one possible path of massive stellar evolution.

Not every star becomes a black hole. Sun-like stars become white dwarfs; some massive stars leave neutron stars; sufficiently massive collapsing cores can form black holes.

Chapter five • The Planck Scale

Where our present theories need something deeper

The Planck length is about 1.6 × 10⁻³⁵ meter. It is not simply “the smallest distance we can measure.” It marks a scale where quantum physics and gravity are expected to need a deeper combined theory.
